## You may

- Use the name "hot-updater" to refer to this project in plain text, for example in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, and documentation.
- Name a plugin, adapter, or integration for hot-updater after it, including a paid or closed-source one, for example `hot-updater-plugin-<name>` or `@your-scope/hot-updater-<name>`. Describe it as a plugin for hot-updater, not as part of hot-updater.
- Say that your product, fork, or hosted service is built on hot-updater, using wording such as:
  - "Built on hot-updater"
  - "Powered by hot-updater"
  - "Based on hot-updater"
  - "Compatible with hot-updater"
- Link to https://github.com/gronxb/hot-updater or https://hot-updater.dev.
- Use the logo, unmodified, when writing about or linking to this project itself (for example in a blog post, talk slide, or "integrations" list).

We encourage forks and hosted services to include one of the credit lines above.

## You may not

- Offer hot-updater, or a service built on it, as a hosted service or SaaS under the hot-updater name or logo, for example a "hot-updater Cloud" or a hot-updater-branded dashboard.
- Use "hot-updater" (or a confusingly similar name) as the name of your hosted service, fork, company, or domain. Plugin names as described above are the only exception.
- Use the hot-updater logo, or a logo that is identical or confusingly similar to it, as the logo or icon of your own product, fork, service, or plugin.
- Present your product, service, or plugin in a way that suggests it is the official hot-updater, or that it is endorsed by or affiliated with the hot-updater project, unless you have written permission. The `hot-updater` npm package and the `@hot-updater` npm scope are the official packages.
- Modify the logo and use it to represent something other than this project.

## Forks and hosted services

You are welcome to fork hot-updater and to build commercial products or hosted services on it under the MIT License. When you do, please:

1. Keep the MIT copyright and license notice, as the license requires.
2. Use your own name and your own logo.
3. Credit the project with a line such as "Built on hot-updater".

## Plugins

Anyone may publish a plugin for hot-updater, free or paid, under a name that includes "hot-updater" as described above. A plugin that connects to a hosted service of yours, such as your own analytics or storage cloud, can carry the hot-updater name; the service itself uses your own name.
