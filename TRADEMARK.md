The Marks belong to Sungyu Kang ([@gronxb](https://github.com/gronxb)). This policy explains how you can and cannot use them. It exists so that no product, service, fork, or company presents itself as hot-updater or passes hot-updater off as its own work, while hosted services and plugin authors stay free to build on it.

## You may

- Use the name "hot-updater" to refer to this project in plain text, for example in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, and documentation.
- Name a plugin or adapter for hot-updater after it, including a paid or closed-source one, for example `hot-updater-plugin-<name>`, `hot-updater-adapter-<name>`, or `@your-scope/hot-updater-<name>`. Describe it as a plugin or adapter for hot-updater, not as part of hot-updater.
- Say that your product, fork, or hosted service is built on hot-updater, using wording such as:
  - "Built on hot-updater"
  - "Powered by hot-updater"
  - "Based on hot-updater"
  - "Compatible with hot-updater"
- Use the name and the unmodified logo to refer to the project, for example beside "Powered by hot-updater" or "Built on hot-updater", or on an integrations, compatibility, or comparison page. It must be clearly hot-updater's mark, and no more prominent than your own branding.
- Link to https://github.com/gronxb/hot-updater or https://hot-updater.dev.

## You may not

- Use the hot-updater name or logo as, or as part of, the branding of your own product, service, company, or domain, for example a "hot-updater Cloud" or the logo as your homepage's or app's own mark. Plugin and adapter names as described above are the only exception for the name.
- Present hot-updater, or a product built on it, as your own creation.
- Suggest that your product, service, plugin, or adapter is the official hot-updater, or that it is endorsed by or affiliated with the hot-updater project, unless you have written permission. The `hot-updater` npm package and the `@hot-updater` npm scope are the official packages.
- Modify the logo and use it to represent something other than this project.

## Hosted services and SaaS

You are welcome to build commercial products and hosted services on hot-updater. When you do:

1. Use your own name and your own logo.
2. Credit the project with a line such as "Powered by hot-updater", linked to the project. For `@hot-updater/server` and `@hot-updater/console`, their LICENSE requires it.
3. Keep the copyright and license notices, as the licenses require.

## Forks

You are welcome to fork hot-updater. Keep the copyright and license notices, use your own name and your own logo, and credit the project with a line such as "Built on hot-updater".

## Plugins and adapters

Anyone may publish a plugin or adapter for hot-updater, free or paid, under a name that includes "hot-updater" as described above. A plugin or adapter that connects to a hosted service of yours, such as your own analytics or storage cloud, can carry the hot-updater name; the service itself uses your own name.
