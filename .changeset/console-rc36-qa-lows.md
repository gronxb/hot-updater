The Console's channel filters now read "All channels" on Bundles and Native builds alike. A build page's "Native builds" link goes back to the list of the build's own channel, where its row is, instead of the production list; while the build loads, can't be read, or isn't found, it goes to every channel's list. On a phone, every dropdown option is a 44 px row. A dropdown is as wide as its longest option, within the screen, so a long channel name shows whole instead of being cut off at the popup's edge.
