The sandbox Home leads with what the build is for. Under the current bundle come **Channels**, with **Scan a channel QR code** and **Enter a channel**, then **Downloaded**. The dev server is one compact card at the end. Closed, it's a **Dev server** row that shows a saved host that answered; open, it lists the hosts used before, takes a new address with **Open**, and links to **Help**. While a dev server runs, the card is a single row with its address, which reloads, and **Exit**. iOS and Android match.

The screen also reads at WCAG AA. Orange text, the focus ring, the progress bar and the selected tab use a darker orange in light mode. Labels on orange buttons are near-black. Placeholders and the unselected tab use the secondary text color. With Reduce Motion on iOS, or animations off on Android, cards open and close without the spring. Dialogs that only show a message close with **Done** instead of **OK**.
