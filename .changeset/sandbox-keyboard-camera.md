Sandbox screen fixes. Opening **Enter a channel** or **Enter URL manually** now scrolls the field and its button above the on-screen keyboard, on iOS and Android. With camera access off, the iOS QR scanner no longer shows the viewfinder and the aiming hint: it shows one line and an **Open Settings** button, and reads the camera access again when the app comes back to the foreground. A link opened before any bundle runs no longer says it replaces the running bundle. The Geist license reads as paragraphs, without the file's hard line breaks, on both platforms.
