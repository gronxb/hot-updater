Insights adds **Update failures** under Release health, for its channel, platform, Release ID, and period: failed updates and the failure rate of update attempts, failed installations, the patch fallback rate, a channel's failed checks and their rate against its active installations, failures by stage and reason with their HTTP statuses, origin codes, transports, and resources, and recoveries by exit reason. The bundle detail shows **Download failures** since the release's first report, with their rate, including through `standaloneRepository`. Event lists and installation history show update failures with their stage and reason, how a download arrived, and why a crashed process exited. Installations show their user ID only.
