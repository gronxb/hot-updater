`native-build ios` and `native-build android` print a few lines with live progress. One `Preparing` line covers the prebuild, the app version or fingerprint, codegen, Ruby gems, and pods, showing the current sub-step, and ends as `Prepared iOS 1.7.0 · prebuild · codegen · pods` with its duration. The cache result is one line with its reason, such as `Cache miss · engine changed`. Archive, export, and Gradle each show one live line with the phase read from the tool's output (`Compiling React-Core · 412 files`, `Linking`, `Signing`, `Bundling JavaScript`, or Gradle's current task) and the elapsed time, and each ends as one line with its duration. Registering the build and uploading it to TestFlight or Google Play are also one line each. The run ends with a summary: the artifact's path and size, the version and build number, the MIN_BUNDLE_ID, the channel, the total time, and the build log. The xcodebuild or Gradle command line, the full native build key, and what changed since the cached build go to the build log, so credential file paths stay off the terminal. iOS writes the archive and its export to one log. In CI and without a terminal, no spinner runs: each finished step prints one line. A failure ends its step's line as failed, then prints up to 20 of the tool's error lines (an export's errors with the lines that explain them), or else its last 15 lines, and the log's path on stderr. A failed `pod install`, `bundle install`, or codegen names the command and the last lines of its output. `xcbeautify` is no longer used. `expo prebuild` now reports done after it finishes, not before it starts. `@hot-updater/cli-tools` exports `startStep`, `createStep`, `withStep`, `failActiveStep`, `formatDuration`, and `withDuration`; while a step runs, `p` spinners and info lines become its detail, and other logs print after its line. `BuildLogger` takes a `createPhaseReader` instead of `importantLogPatterns` and `progressStages`.
