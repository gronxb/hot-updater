A native build's download no longer competes with how it installs. The Native builds table drops its Download button from every row; a row still opens the build's page. On the build's page, only an APK, which installs from its file, keeps a Download button. An IPA, an AAB, or a simulator app, and a build a store took, shows the download as a small muted link under the card's actions, so "Open App Store Connect" or "Open Play Console" is the one button on a store build's card.
