`@hot-updater/server` and `@hot-updater/console` are now licensed under the MIT License with a hosted service attribution condition. A service that offers either of them, or a service built on them, to other people for updating their own apps must show "Powered by hot-updater" with a link where its users can see it. Running them for your own apps, or for apps you build for clients, needs no notice. The Console's sidebar now shows "Powered by hot-updater", and keeping it satisfies the condition. Every other package stays under the MIT License, and versions released before this one keep their MIT License.
